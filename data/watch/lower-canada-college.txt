Discover All You Can Be... We'll Help
Finding the right fit for something as important as your child’s education means selecting a school that welcomes them, fosters a sense of belonging, and provides a place where your child feels respected and is encouraged to learn, grow and thrive.
We invite you to discover our school and to decide for yourself if LCC is that place.
Join us for a variety of virtual and in-person opportunities to learn about our school. We hope that through our various events you will get a true sense of who we are and what differentiates us, both as a school and as a community.
